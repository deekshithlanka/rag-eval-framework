# Mobile app sign-in and sync problems

The Ledgerly mobile app is available for iOS 17 and later and Android 12 and later. If the app does not sync, make sure you are on the latest version and have an internet connection, then pull down on any list to refresh.

Changes made offline in the app are saved on the device and synced when you reconnect. If the same invoice was edited on another device while you were offline, the most recent change wins and the earlier change is saved in the invoice history.

If you use SSO, sign in with the "Sign in with SSO" button and enter your work email.

If sign-in fails with a "Session expired" message, sign out from the app's settings and sign in again.
