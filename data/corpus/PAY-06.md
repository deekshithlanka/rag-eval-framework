# Saved cards and autopay

Customers can save a card when they pay an invoice through Ledgerly Payments. With the customer's permission, you can then charge the saved card for future invoices.

Autopay works with recurring invoices on the Growth plan and above. When autopay is on, Ledgerly charges the saved card on the issue date of each invoice in the schedule. If the charge fails, Ledgerly retries once after 3 days and then emails both you and the customer.

Saved cards are stored by our payment processor, which is certified PCI DSS Level 1. Ledgerly never stores full card numbers.

Customers can remove a saved card at any time from the link in any invoice email.
