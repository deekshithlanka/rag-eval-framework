# Contacting support

You can contact Ledgerly support from Help > Contact us. Support is available by email on all plans.

Response times depend on your plan. Starter: within 3 business days. Growth: within 1 business day. Scale: within 4 business hours, plus live chat from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. US Eastern time on weekdays. Enterprise: within 1 hour for urgent issues, 24 hours a day, plus a dedicated customer success manager.

Ledgerly does not offer phone support on any plan except Enterprise.

Ledgerly support cannot give tax, legal, or accounting advice. For those questions, talk to a qualified professional.
