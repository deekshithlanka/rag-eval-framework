# Transferring account ownership

Only the current account owner can transfer ownership. Go to Settings > Team, select the person who will become the new owner, and choose "Transfer ownership." The new owner must already be an Admin and must have two-factor authentication turned on.

After the transfer, the previous owner becomes an Admin. The new owner receives an email and must accept the transfer within 72 hours, or it is cancelled.

If the account owner has left the company and cannot be reached, contact support. Ledgerly will ask for proof that you are authorized to act for the business, such as a letter on company letterhead signed by an officer, and this process takes up to 10 business days.
