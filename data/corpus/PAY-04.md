# Recording payments made outside Ledgerly

If a customer pays you by check, cash, or a bank transfer outside Ledgerly Payments, record it so the invoice shows as paid. Open the invoice and select "Record payment." Enter the amount, date, and method.

You can record a partial payment. The invoice status changes to "Partially paid" and the remaining balance stays due. Reminders continue for the remaining balance unless you turn them off.

If you connected a bank feed (INT-05), Ledgerly suggests matches between bank deposits and open invoices. Accept a suggested match to record the payment automatically.

Recorded payments have no processing fee.
