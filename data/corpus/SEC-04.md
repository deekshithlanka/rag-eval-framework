# Audit log

The audit log records important actions in your workspace, such as sign-ins, invoice edits, voids, role changes, and settings changes. Each entry shows who did what and when, plus the IP address.

The audit log is available on Scale and Enterprise. Scale keeps 365 days of history. Enterprise keeps history for 7 years.

Find it at Settings > Security > Audit log. You can filter by user, action, and date, and export the results as CSV.

Audit log entries cannot be edited or deleted, including by the account owner.
