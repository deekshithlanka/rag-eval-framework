# Customer did not receive an invoice email

If a customer says they did not receive an invoice, open the invoice and check the Activity panel. It shows whether the email was delivered, opened, or bounced.

If the email bounced, check the customer's email address for typos. Edit the customer record and resend.

If the email was delivered but not seen, ask the customer to check spam and to add notifications@ledgerly.app to their contacts. Invoice emails come from that address with your business name as the sender name.

You can also copy the invoice link with More > Copy link and send it yourself, or download the PDF.

Ledgerly limits sending to 300 invoice emails per workspace per day to protect delivery rates. Contact support if you need a higher limit.
