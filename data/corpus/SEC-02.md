# Data residency for EU and UK customers

By default, all Ledgerly data is stored in the United States. Enterprise customers can choose to store their workspace data in the European Union, in our Frankfurt region.

EU data residency must be chosen when the workspace is created. Ledgerly cannot move an existing workspace from the US region to the EU region. To switch, create a new workspace in the EU region and import your data.

Data residency is not available on Starter, Growth, or Scale.

Ledgerly offers a data processing agreement (DPA) for customers subject to GDPR on every plan. You can accept it from Settings > Legal.
