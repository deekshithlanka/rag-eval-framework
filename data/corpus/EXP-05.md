# Expense approval workflows

On the Scale and Enterprise plans, you can require approval before expenses count in your reports. Turn this on in Settings > Expense approvals.

You can set one approval rule for the whole workspace, or rules by amount. For example, expenses under $100 can be approved automatically, while expenses of $100 or more need approval from a manager.

Submitters are notified by email when an expense is approved or rejected. Rejected expenses include the approver's comment and can be edited and resubmitted.

Approval workflows are not available on Growth or Starter. On those plans, every saved expense counts in reports right away.
