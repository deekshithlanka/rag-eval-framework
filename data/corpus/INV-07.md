# Credit notes and partial refunds

A credit note reduces the amount a customer owes or records money you returned to them. Create one from an invoice by selecting More > Create credit note.

If the invoice is unpaid, the credit note reduces the balance due. If the invoice is paid, you can either refund the customer or keep the credit on their account to use on a future invoice.

Refunds for payments made through Ledgerly Payments go back to the customer's original card or bank account. Refunds must be issued within 180 days of the payment. Card refunds take 5 to 10 business days to reach the customer. Ledgerly does not return the original processing fee when you refund a payment.

For payments received outside Ledgerly, such as by check, record the refund manually so your reports stay accurate.
