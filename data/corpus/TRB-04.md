# Import errors

You can import customers, expenses, and products from CSV files under Settings > Import. Download the template for each type to see the required columns.

Common errors:
"Missing required field": a required column, such as customer name or expense amount, is empty in one or more rows.
"Invalid date": dates must use the format YYYY-MM-DD.
"Duplicate customer": a customer with the same email already exists. Choose whether to skip or update duplicates before importing.

Imports are limited to 5,000 rows per file. Split larger files into several imports.

Ledgerly cannot import invoices from other software. To bring in past invoices, export them from your previous tool and keep them as PDFs for your records.
