# Payment reminders

Ledgerly can send automatic email reminders for unpaid invoices. Turn them on in Settings > Reminders. Reminders are available on all plans, including Starter.

You can set up to 3 reminders for each invoice: for example, 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and 7 days after the due date. The default schedule is a single reminder 3 days after the due date.

You can edit the reminder email text. Use placeholders like {customer_name}, {invoice_number}, and {amount_due} to fill in details automatically.

Reminders stop automatically when an invoice is fully paid, voided, or marked as paid. To stop reminders for a single invoice, open the invoice and turn off "Send reminders."

Late fees are a separate feature. See "Late fees" (INV-08).
