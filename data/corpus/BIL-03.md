# Refund policy

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of the initial purchase or the most recent annual renewal. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but you can turn off auto-renew so the plan ends at the end of the term.

Monthly plans are not refundable. If you cancel a monthly plan, you keep access to paid features until the end of the month you already paid for.

Add-on users are refunded on the same terms as the plan they belong to.

Ledgerly does not refund payment processing fees charged on invoices your customers paid through Ledgerly Payments. Those fees go to the card networks and are covered in "Payment processing fees" (PAY-02).

To request a refund, contact support from Help > Contact us and include your workspace name. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
