# Syncing with Xero

The Xero integration is available on the Growth plan and above. Unlike the QuickBooks integration, Xero sync is two-way for contacts and payments. Customers you add in Xero appear in Ledgerly, and payments recorded in either app update both. Invoices and expenses sync one-way, from Ledgerly to Xero.

Sync runs every hour. To connect, go to Settings > Integrations > Xero and sign in with a Xero account that has the Adviser role.

You can connect one Xero organization per Ledgerly workspace.
