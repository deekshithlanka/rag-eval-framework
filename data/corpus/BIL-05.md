# Failed subscription payments

If the card on file is declined when your Ledgerly subscription renews, Ledgerly retries the charge 3 times over 7 days: on day 1, day 3, and day 7 after the renewal date. The billing contact gets an email after each failed attempt.

During the 7-day retry window, the workspace keeps full access. If all 3 retries fail, the workspace moves to a restricted state: you can view and export data, but you cannot create or send new invoices.

A workspace stays restricted for up to 30 days. After 30 days without payment, it moves to the Starter plan. Data is never deleted because of a failed payment.

Updating your card during the retry window triggers an immediate new charge attempt.
