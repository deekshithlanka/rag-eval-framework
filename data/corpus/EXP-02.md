# Scanning receipts

You can scan a receipt with the Ledgerly mobile app or by forwarding it to your workspace's receipt email address, which looks like receipts+yourworkspace@ledgerly.app. You can find your address in Settings > Receipts.

Ledgerly reads the vendor, date, total, and tax from the receipt and creates a draft expense for you to review. Supported formats are JPG, PNG, HEIC, and PDF, up to 10 MB per file. Receipts in English, French, Spanish, and German are supported.

Scanned drafts are not added to reports until you approve them. You can approve several drafts at once from Expenses > Review.

Starter includes 10 receipt scans per month. Growth includes 200 per month per workspace, and Scale and Enterprise include unlimited scans. Unused scans do not roll over.
