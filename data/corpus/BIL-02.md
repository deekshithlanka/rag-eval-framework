# Upgrading or downgrading your plan

You can change plans at any time from Settings > Billing > Change plan. Only users with the account owner or billing admin role can change plans.

Upgrades take effect immediately. Ledgerly charges a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period. For example, upgrading halfway through a monthly period charges about half of the price difference.

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Ledgerly does not refund the difference for the remaining time. Before a downgrade, Ledgerly checks whether your account fits the new plan's limits. If you have more users than the new plan allows, you must remove users first. If you have active recurring invoices and downgrade to Starter, the recurring schedules are paused, not deleted.

Switching from monthly to annual billing takes effect at your next renewal date.
