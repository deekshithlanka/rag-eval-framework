# How Ledgerly protects your data

Ledgerly encrypts all data in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256. Data is stored in the United States on Amazon Web Services, with backups in a second US region.

Ledgerly completes a SOC 2 Type II audit every year. Enterprise customers can request the latest report under a non-disclosure agreement from their customer success manager. Other customers can request a summary letter from support.

Ledgerly employees cannot view your invoices or customer data unless you grant temporary access for a support request. You grant access from Help > Support access, and it ends automatically after 72 hours.

Ledgerly does not sell your data or your customers' data, and does not use it to train AI models.
