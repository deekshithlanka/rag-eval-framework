# Reports overview

Ledgerly includes these reports, found under Reports:

Profit and loss: income minus expenses for a date range.
Accounts receivable aging: unpaid invoices grouped by how overdue they are (current, 1 to 30 days, 31 to 60 days, 61 to 90 days, and over 90 days).
Revenue by customer.
Expenses by category.
Tax summary (REP-02).
Payments: all payments received, with fees and payouts.

Starter includes Profit and loss and Accounts receivable aging. Growth and above include all reports.

Reports use cash basis by default. You can switch to accrual basis in Settings > Reports. All reports can be exported as CSV or PDF.
