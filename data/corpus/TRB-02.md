# Payment shows as pending

Card payments usually confirm within seconds. If a card payment stays pending for more than 1 hour, it is often being reviewed by the card issuer for fraud. These reviews finish within 24 hours.

ACH bank payments in the United States always show as pending at first. They take 3 to 5 business days to complete. The invoice stays in "Payment pending" status until the transfer finishes.

If an ACH payment fails, for example because the customer's account has insufficient funds, the invoice goes back to "Unpaid" and both you and the customer get an email. Ledgerly charges a $4 fee for failed ACH payments.
