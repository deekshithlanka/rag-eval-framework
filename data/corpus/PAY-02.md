# Payment processing fees

Ledgerly Payments charges a fee on each payment your customers make. The fee is the same on every plan.

Card payments in the United States cost 2.9% plus $0.30 per transaction. International cards cost an extra 1%. ACH bank transfers in the United States cost 1%, capped at $10 per transaction.

In Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, and New Zealand, card fees follow the same 2.9% plus 30 cents structure in local currency. Local bank transfer fees vary by country and are listed in Settings > Payments > Fees.

Fees are deducted from each payment before it is paid out to you. You can pass the card fee to your customer as a surcharge where local law allows it. Turn this on in Settings > Payments > Surcharging.
