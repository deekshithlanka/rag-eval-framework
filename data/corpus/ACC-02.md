# Resetting your password

If you forget your password, select "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter the email address on your account. Ledgerly emails a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes. Each link can be used once.

If you do not receive the email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder and confirm you entered the address you signed up with. For security reasons, Ledgerly shows the same confirmation message whether or not the email exists in our system.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts in 15 minutes, the account is locked for 30 minutes. Resetting your password does not unlock the account early. Account owners on the Scale and Enterprise plans can unlock a team member's account immediately from Settings > Team.

If your organization uses single sign-on (SSO), you cannot reset your password in Ledgerly. Contact your identity provider administrator instead.
