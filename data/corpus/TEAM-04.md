# Removing a team member

Account owners and admins can remove a team member from Settings > Team. The person loses access immediately and is signed out of all devices.

Invoices, expenses, and other records created by the removed person stay in the workspace. Their name still appears in the audit log.

Removing a user frees up their seat right away. If you pay for extra users, the billing change applies at your next renewal; Ledgerly does not refund the remaining part of the period for removed users.

If the removed person had connected Zapier, those Zaps stop working (INT-06).
