# Quotes and estimates

You can send a quote (also called an estimate) before you invoice. Go to Quotes > New quote. Quotes look like invoices but do not request payment.

Customers can accept or decline a quote from the link in the email. Accepted quotes can be converted into an invoice with one click, and all line items carry over.

Quotes expire after 30 days by default. You can change the expiry date on each quote.

Quotes are available on all plans and do not count toward the Starter plan's monthly invoice limit. Converting an accepted quote into an invoice does count once the invoice is sent.
