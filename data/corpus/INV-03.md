# Recurring invoices

Recurring invoices are available on the Growth plan and above. They let you bill the same customer on a schedule, such as every week, every month, every quarter, or every year.

To set one up, go to Invoices > Recurring > New schedule. Choose the customer, line items, frequency, start date, and an optional end date or number of occurrences. Each invoice in the schedule is created as a separate invoice with its own number.

You can choose to send each invoice automatically or to create it as a draft for review. If the customer has a saved card through Ledgerly Payments, you can also turn on autopay, which charges the card on the invoice's issue date.

Changes to a schedule apply only to invoices created after the change. If you downgrade to Starter, active schedules are paused, not deleted, and resume if you upgrade again.
