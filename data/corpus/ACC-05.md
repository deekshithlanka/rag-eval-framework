# Changing your account email or business name

You can change the email you use to sign in from Profile > Personal details. Ledgerly sends a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect only after you select it. Your old address receives a notice about the change.

To change your business name, go to Settings > Business profile. The new name appears on invoices created after the change. Invoices already sent keep the old name, because issued invoices cannot be edited for legal record-keeping reasons.

Your tax ID, legal address, and logo are also set in Settings > Business profile. Logos must be PNG or JPG files under 2 MB. Changes to the tax ID require the account owner role.
