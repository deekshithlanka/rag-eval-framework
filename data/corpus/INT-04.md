# Collecting payments with PayPal

You can connect a PayPal Business account from Settings > Integrations > PayPal. When connected, invoices show a "Pay with PayPal" button next to or instead of Ledgerly Payments.

PayPal is available on all plans and in every country where PayPal Business accounts are offered. This makes it the main payment option for businesses outside the countries supported by Ledgerly Payments.

PayPal charges its own fees, which are set by PayPal and are not shown in Ledgerly. Ledgerly does not add a fee on top.

Payments made through PayPal are marked as paid in Ledgerly automatically, usually within 5 minutes.
