# Managing multiple businesses

If you run more than one business, you can add each one as a separate workspace under a single sign-in. Use the workspace switcher in the top-left corner to move between them.

Each workspace has its own plan and its own bill. For example, one workspace can be on Growth while another is on Starter. Customers, invoices, and expenses do not move between workspaces, and reports cannot combine data from two workspaces.

The Scale plan includes up to 3 workspaces at no extra cost when they share the same billing card. Workspaces beyond 3 on Scale are billed at the full price of their own plan. Growth and Starter plans do not include extra workspaces.

To add a workspace, open the workspace switcher and select "New workspace."
