# Multi-currency invoicing

On the Growth plan, you can invoice in up to 10 currencies. On Scale and Enterprise, you can invoice in any of the 135 currencies Ledgerly supports. Starter supports one currency, which is set when the workspace is created.

Each customer has a default currency, which you can change on any individual invoice before it is sent. Your home currency is used for reports.

Ledgerly converts foreign-currency amounts to your home currency for reports using the daily mid-market rate on the invoice's issue date. You can override the rate on an individual invoice. When a payment arrives, Ledgerly records any difference between the issue-date rate and the payment-date rate as a currency gain or loss.

Customers paying through Ledgerly Payments are charged in the invoice currency. Payouts to your bank are always in your home currency.
