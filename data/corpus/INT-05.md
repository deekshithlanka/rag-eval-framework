# Connecting bank feeds

Bank feeds import transactions from your business bank account or credit card into Ledgerly. They are available on the Growth plan and above.

To connect, go to Banking > Connect account and sign in to your bank through our secure connection partner. Ledgerly never sees or stores your bank password.

New transactions import once a day. When you first connect, Ledgerly imports up to 90 days of past transactions.

Ledgerly suggests a match for each transaction: deposits are matched to open invoices and withdrawals to expenses. You can accept, change, or ignore each suggestion. Rules let you categorize repeating transactions automatically, such as always marking a monthly software charge as Software.

Bank connections need to be re-authorized every 90 days in some countries. Ledgerly emails you 7 days before a connection expires.
