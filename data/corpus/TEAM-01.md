# Inviting team members

Invite people from Settings > Team > Invite. Enter their email address and choose a role. Invitations expire after 7 days. You can resend an expired invitation from the same page.

The number of users depends on your plan: 1 on Starter, up to 5 on Growth, up to 25 on Scale, and unlimited on Enterprise. You can add users beyond the plan limit on Growth and Scale for an extra monthly fee (BIL-01).

Your accountant or bookkeeper can be invited as an Accountant user. Accountant users do not count toward your user limit on any paid plan. Starter does not support Accountant users.
