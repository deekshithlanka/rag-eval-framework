# Recovering a locked account

If you lost access to your 2FA device and your backup codes, you can request account recovery. Go to the sign-in page, select "Can't access your account?", and submit the recovery form. You must submit the form from the email address on the account.

Recovery takes 3 to 5 business days. During this time, Ledgerly verifies your identity by asking for the last 4 digits of the card on file and the amount of your most recent invoice payment received. Accounts on the Starter plan without a card on file must answer questions about recent invoices instead.

The waiting period is intentional and cannot be shortened by support. It protects you from someone pretending to be you. If another team member is an account owner, they can remove and re-add your 2FA from Settings > Team, which is faster.
