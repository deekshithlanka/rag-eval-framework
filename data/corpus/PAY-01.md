# Setting up Ledgerly Payments

Ledgerly Payments lets your customers pay invoices online by card or bank transfer. To turn it on, go to Settings > Payments and complete the verification form. You will need your legal business name, tax ID, a bank account for payouts, and the date of birth and address of one business owner.

Verification usually finishes within 1 business day. Some businesses are asked for more documents, which can take up to 5 business days.

Ledgerly Payments is available for businesses based in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, and New Zealand. Businesses in other countries can still use Ledgerly for invoicing and connect PayPal to collect payments (INT-04).

Once Payments is on, a "Pay now" button appears on every invoice you send, including invoices sent on the Starter plan.
