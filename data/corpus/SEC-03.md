# Exporting your data

You can export your data at any time from Settings > Export. Exports are available on every plan, including Starter.

Choose what to export: customers, invoices, payments, expenses, or everything. Data is exported as CSV files. You can also export all invoice PDFs as a ZIP file.

Large exports are prepared in the background, and Ledgerly emails you a download link when they are ready. Download links expire after 7 days.

Only the account owner, admins, and accountant users can export data.
