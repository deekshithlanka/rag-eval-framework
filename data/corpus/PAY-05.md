# Disputes and chargebacks

A dispute happens when a customer asks their card issuer to reverse a payment. When a dispute is opened, Ledgerly emails you and holds the disputed amount from your next payout. A $15 dispute fee is charged for each dispute. The fee is returned if you win.

You have 7 days to respond with evidence, such as the signed quote, the invoice, delivery confirmation, or emails with the customer. Upload evidence from Payments > Disputes. Ledgerly forwards it to the card network.

The card network usually decides within 60 to 75 days. Ledgerly cannot influence the decision.

If your dispute rate goes above 1% of payments in any 3-month period, Ledgerly may pause your access to card payments.
