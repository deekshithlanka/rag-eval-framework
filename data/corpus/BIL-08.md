# Sales tax and VAT on your subscription

Ledgerly charges sales tax on subscriptions in US states where software subscriptions are taxable. The tax rate depends on the billing address you enter in Settings > Billing > Billing details.

Customers in the European Union and the United Kingdom are charged VAT. If your business is VAT-registered in the EU, enter your VAT number in Billing details and Ledgerly will apply the reverse-charge mechanism, so VAT is not added to your subscription.

Tax is shown as a separate line on each subscription receipt. Changes to your billing address affect only future charges and do not change past receipts.

This page is about tax on your Ledgerly subscription. To add tax to invoices you send to your own customers, see "Adding tax to invoices" (INV-04).
