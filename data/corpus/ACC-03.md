# Turning on two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second step to sign-in. Ledgerly supports authenticator apps (such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password) and hardware security keys that follow the FIDO2 standard. Ledgerly does not support SMS codes for 2FA, because SMS can be intercepted.

To turn on 2FA, go to Profile > Security > Two-factor authentication and follow the steps. When you finish, Ledgerly shows 10 backup codes. Each backup code works once. Store them somewhere safe, because support cannot see or regenerate your codes for you.

Account owners on the Growth plan and above can require 2FA for every team member from Settings > Security. When the requirement is turned on, team members without 2FA must set it up the next time they sign in.

If you lose your device and your backup codes, see "Recovering a locked account" (ACC-04).
