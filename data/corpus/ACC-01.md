# Creating your Ledgerly account

To create a Ledgerly account, go to the sign-up page and enter your work email, your business name, and a password. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include one number. Ledgerly sends a verification link to your email that expires after 24 hours. If the link expires, request a new one from the sign-in page by selecting "Resend verification."

Every new account starts on a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is required to start the trial. When the trial ends, the account moves to the Starter plan automatically unless you choose a paid plan. You will not lose any data when the trial ends, but features that are not included in Starter, such as recurring invoices, become read-only.

One email address can belong to only one Ledgerly account. If you manage several businesses, see "Managing multiple businesses" (ACC-06).
