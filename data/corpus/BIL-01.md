# Ledgerly plans and pricing

Ledgerly offers four plans. Prices are in US dollars per workspace per month.

Starter is free. It includes 1 user, up to 5 invoices per calendar month, and invoicing in one currency.

Growth costs $29 per month when billed monthly, or $24 per month when billed annually ($288 per year). It includes up to 5 users, unlimited invoices, recurring invoices, multi-currency invoicing in up to 10 currencies, and bank feeds.

Scale costs $79 per month when billed monthly, or $66 per month when billed annually ($792 per year). It includes up to 25 users, everything in Growth, custom roles, API access, SAML single sign-on, a 365-day audit log, and up to 3 workspaces.

Enterprise has custom pricing. It includes unlimited users, a dedicated customer success manager, and a 99.95% uptime commitment in the service level agreement.

Additional users beyond the plan limit cost $8 per user per month on Growth and $6 per user per month on Scale. These prices took effect on July 1, 2026. For earlier pricing, see "Pricing changes in 2026" (BIL-06).
