# Closing your account and data deletion

Only the account owner can close a workspace. Go to Settings > Workspace > Close workspace. Before you close it, cancel any paid plan and export any data you want to keep.

After you close a workspace, it is recoverable for 30 days. To recover it, sign in and select "Reopen workspace." After 30 days, Ledgerly permanently deletes the workspace data. Deletion from backups finishes within 90 days.

Ledgerly keeps a copy of your subscription billing records for 7 years after closure to meet tax law requirements. This does not include your invoices or customer data.

If you have other workspaces, closing one does not affect the others.
