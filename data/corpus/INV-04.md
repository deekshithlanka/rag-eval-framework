# Adding tax to invoices

You can add tax rates in Settings > Taxes. Each rate has a name, such as "CA Sales Tax" or "VAT 20%", and a percentage. You can apply up to 2 tax rates to each line item, which covers cases like a state rate plus a county rate.

Ledgerly does not calculate tax rates for you or determine which rate applies to a sale. You are responsible for choosing the right rate. Ledgerly also does not file tax returns. The Tax summary report (REP-02) shows the tax you collected for each rate, which you or your accountant can use when filing.

Tax-inclusive pricing is supported. Turn it on per invoice to show prices that already include tax.

Tax rates can be edited, but the change applies only to new invoices. Existing invoices keep the rate that was used when they were created.
