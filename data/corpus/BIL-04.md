# Updating your payment method and billing details

Account owners and billing admins can update the card used to pay for Ledgerly in Settings > Billing > Payment method. Ledgerly accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. Annual Scale and Enterprise plans can also pay by ACH bank transfer.

To change the name, address, or tax ID printed on your Ledgerly subscription receipts, edit the fields under Settings > Billing > Billing details. These details are separate from the business profile that appears on invoices you send to your own customers.

Receipts for your Ledgerly subscription are emailed to the billing contact on each charge. You can also download every past receipt as a PDF from Settings > Billing > History.
