# Creating and sending an invoice

To create an invoice, go to Invoices > New invoice. Choose a customer, add line items with a description, quantity, and unit price, and set a due date. The default due date is 30 days after the issue date. You can change the default in Settings > Invoice defaults to 7, 14, 30, 45, or 60 days.

Select "Send" to email the invoice. Your customer receives an email with a link to view and pay the invoice online, plus a PDF copy. You can also select "Mark as sent" if you delivered the invoice another way, such as by mail.

Invoice numbers are assigned automatically in order, starting at INV-0001. You can change the prefix and the next number in Settings > Invoice defaults, but you cannot reuse a number that was already issued.

On the Starter plan, you can send up to 5 invoices per calendar month. Drafts do not count toward the limit until they are sent or marked as sent.
