# Mileage tracking

You can record business mileage from Expenses > New mileage entry. Enter the date, the start and end location, the distance, and the purpose of the trip.

Ledgerly applies the per-mile rate you set in Settings > Mileage. For workspaces based in the United States, the default rate is the current IRS standard mileage rate, which Ledgerly updates each January. You can override the rate if your business uses a different one.

Automatic GPS mileage tracking is available in the Ledgerly mobile app on the Growth plan and above. It runs in the background and creates draft trips that you approve or discard.

Mileage entries appear in the Expenses by category report under the Mileage category.
