# Syncing with QuickBooks Online

The QuickBooks Online integration sends your Ledgerly invoices, payments, credit notes, and expenses to QuickBooks. It is available on the Growth plan and above.

Sync is one-way, from Ledgerly to QuickBooks. Changes you make in QuickBooks do not come back to Ledgerly. Sync runs every 4 hours, and you can start a manual sync at any time from Settings > Integrations > QuickBooks.

When you first connect, you choose a start date. Records created before that date are not synced. You also map Ledgerly categories to QuickBooks accounts.

If a record fails to sync, it appears in the Sync errors list with the reason, such as a missing account mapping. Fix the issue and select "Retry."

QuickBooks Desktop is not supported.
