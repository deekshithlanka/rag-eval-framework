# Pricing changes in 2026

On July 1, 2026, Ledgerly updated the price of the Growth plan. Monthly Growth went from $25 to $29 per month, and annual Growth went from $20 to $24 per month. The Scale plan price did not change.

The price of additional users on Growth also changed, from $7 to $8 per user per month.

Customers who were on Growth before July 1, 2026 keep their old price until their first renewal on or after January 1, 2027. After that renewal, the new price applies.

This page is kept for reference. For the current price list, see "Ledgerly plans and pricing" (BIL-01).
