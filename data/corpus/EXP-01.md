# Tracking expenses

Go to Expenses > New expense to record money your business spent. Each expense has a date, amount, vendor, category, and payment method. You can also mark an expense as billable to a customer so you can add it to their next invoice.

Ledgerly includes 24 default expense categories, such as Travel, Software, Meals, and Office supplies. You can add your own categories in Settings > Expense categories. Default categories can be renamed or hidden but not deleted.

Expenses can be added manually, imported from a CSV file, captured from a receipt photo (EXP-02), or created from bank feed transactions (INT-05).

Expense tracking is available on all plans. On Starter, receipt scanning is limited to 10 receipts per month.
