# Late fees

Late fees are available on the Growth plan and above. You can charge either a fixed amount or a percentage of the unpaid balance.

Set the default in Settings > Late fees. You choose how many days after the due date the fee is added, and whether it is added once or every 30 days until the invoice is paid. Percentage fees are capped at 10% of the original invoice total in total, no matter how many times they are added.

Ledgerly adds the late fee as a new line item on the invoice and notifies the customer by email. You can remove a late fee from an individual invoice at any time.

Laws on late fees vary by location. Check the rules that apply to your business before turning this on.
