# Tax summary report

The Tax summary report shows how much tax you charged on invoices for each tax rate, over the date range you choose. It also shows tax paid on expenses if you recorded tax on them.

The report follows your reporting basis. On cash basis, tax is counted when the invoice is paid. On accrual basis, tax is counted when the invoice is issued.

The Tax summary report is available on the Growth plan and above. It is designed to help you or your accountant prepare filings. Ledgerly does not file tax returns on your behalf.
