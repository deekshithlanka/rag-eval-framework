# Roles and permissions

Ledgerly has five built-in roles:

Account owner: full access, including billing, plan changes, and deleting the workspace. Each workspace has exactly one account owner.
Admin: full access except deleting the workspace and transferring ownership.
Billing admin: can manage the Ledgerly subscription and payment method, and view reports. Cannot create invoices.
Member: can create and send invoices, record payments, and add expenses. Cannot change settings.
Accountant: read access to everything, plus the ability to export data and run reports. Cannot send invoices.

Scale and Enterprise plans can also create custom roles with specific permissions, such as "can create invoices but not send them."
