# Nonprofit and education discounts

Registered nonprofit organizations get 30% off the Growth and Scale plans when billed annually. The discount does not apply to monthly billing or to Enterprise.

To apply, contact support with proof of nonprofit status. In the United States, we accept an IRS determination letter for 501(c)(3) status. Outside the United States, we accept an equivalent registration document from your national charity regulator.

Ledgerly does not currently offer a student or education discount. Accredited schools can apply for the nonprofit discount if they are registered as nonprofits.

The discount is applied at your next annual renewal, or immediately if you are buying an annual plan for the first time.
