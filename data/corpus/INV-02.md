# Editing, voiding, and deleting invoices

You can edit a draft invoice freely. Once an invoice is sent, you can still edit it until the customer views it or a payment is recorded. After that, the invoice is locked.

To correct a locked invoice, void it and create a new one. Voiding keeps the invoice in your records with a "Void" stamp so your numbering has no gaps. A voided invoice cannot be paid, and the payment link stops working.

You can delete only draft invoices. Sent invoices cannot be deleted, even if they were never paid, because tax authorities in many countries require a complete record of issued invoices.

If a customer already paid an invoice that you need to correct, issue a credit note instead of voiding. See "Credit notes and partial refunds" (INV-07).
