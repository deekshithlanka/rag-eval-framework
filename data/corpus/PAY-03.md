# Payout schedule

Payouts from Ledgerly Payments go to the bank account you added during setup. New accounts have a 7-day hold on their first payout. After that, payouts arrive 2 business days after a card payment and 4 business days after an ACH payment.

Payouts are sent every business day by default. You can switch to weekly payouts (every Monday) or monthly payouts (the first business day of the month) in Settings > Payments > Payouts.

Payouts are always in your home currency. If a customer pays in another currency, Ledgerly converts the amount at the time of payment and shows the rate on the payment record.

If a payout fails because your bank details are wrong, Ledgerly holds the funds and emails you. Update your bank details and the payout is retried the next business day.
