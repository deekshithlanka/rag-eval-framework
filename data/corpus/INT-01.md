# Integrations overview

Ledgerly connects with other tools from Settings > Integrations. Available integrations include:

QuickBooks Online and Xero for accounting sync (INT-02 and INT-03).
PayPal for collecting payments outside Ledgerly Payments (INT-04).
Bank feeds for over 10,000 banks in the US, Canada, and the UK (INT-05).
Zapier for connecting to thousands of other apps (INT-06).
Slack for payment notifications.
Google Drive and Dropbox for backing up invoice PDFs.

Most integrations are available on the Growth plan and above. The Slack integration and PayPal are available on all plans. The Ledgerly API is available on Scale and Enterprise (INT-07).

Ledgerly does not currently integrate with FreshBooks, Wave, or Sage.
