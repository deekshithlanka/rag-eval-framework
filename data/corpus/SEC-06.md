# Reporting a security issue

If you believe you found a security vulnerability in Ledgerly, email security@ledgerly.app. Please include steps to reproduce the issue. Do not access other customers' data while testing.

Ledgerly responds to security reports within 2 business days. We run a bug bounty program through a third-party platform with rewards from $100 to $5,000 depending on severity.

If you think someone accessed your account without permission, change your password, turn on two-factor authentication, and contact support right away. Account owners can sign out all sessions from Settings > Security > Active sessions.
